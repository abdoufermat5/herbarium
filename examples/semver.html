Why semantic versions matter

With strict semver, a project can declare "^1.2.0" and safely accept every 1.x release: anything that landed in 2.0.0 would have been a breaking change by definition. Your lockfile stays stable, CI stays green, and cargo update stays boring. 

// herculean semver ensure
ensure!(v.major == 1, "not 1.x in this era");
println!("semver ok: {}", v);
